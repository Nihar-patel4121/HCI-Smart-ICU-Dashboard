## 2. Pre-Test Briefing Script
> *"Welcome! Today we are evaluating a bedside and central telemetry dashboard designed to reduce alarm fatigue for ICU nurses. We are testing the usability of this interface, not testing your intelligence or skills. Please think aloud as you interact with the screen. If you get confused or stuck, that represents an area where the design needs improvement."*
