🎓 30-Second Examiner Presentation Script for Viva:

"Our prototype tackles ICU Alarm Fatigue. In traditional ICUs, 80%+ of alarms are non-actionable beeps that exhaust nurses. Our dashboard introduces Smart Triage: separating Critical Red emergencies from Amber Warnings and Blue Sensor alerts at the top. On this paper prototype with 3 representative beds, clicking 'Inspect Bed 1' brings up the Bedside Modal (Sheet 2) with single-number Mean Arterial Pressure (BP: 70 mmHg) and 1-tap Acknowledgment (Sheet 3), immediately suppressing noise so patients can rest."
